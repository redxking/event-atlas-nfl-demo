ASSESSMENT BOUNDARY

Evidence before escalation

Weather and seismic proximity are operational context, not a threat assessment. A KEV listing does not establish that a venue uses the affected product. Named-person assessments require an authorized purpose, corroboration, and human review.

Election sites are source records, not confirmed current voter assignments. New York GIS records date to June 2026. Pennsylvania rows reflect a May 2026 statewide reference, can repeat physical sites by precinct, and have estimated geocoded points. North Carolina November 2026 early-vote sites have published schedules but no source coordinates; its November Election Day file is not yet published. Washington November 2026 drop boxes and voting centers have publisher points but no per-site hours. South Carolina November 2026 early-vote centers have published statewide dates and hours but no points. West Virginia November 2026 early-vote sites preserve county hours and late-opening dates. Delaware November 2026 early-vote and Election Day tables preserve their published schedules and district mappings, without coordinates. Florida November 2026 county PDFs add site-specific early-voting schedules and additional secure ballot intake stations; some source addresses and dates are incomplete. Connecticut November 2026 early-vote sites come from a rolling municipal list without site hours or coordinates. New Jersey November 2026 early-vote sites have county-provided addresses and statewide minimum hours; some publisher addresses omit ZIPs. Source outages can leave retained rows marked stale; inspect each source status. DC dates and hours are publisher claims. Maryland 2026 Election Day GIS sites have coordinates; its early-vote and drop-box PDFs do not. Verify every site with the responsible election office before action.

Election-site coverage

Selected official site lists from NY, DC, MD, PA, NC, WA, SC, WV, DE and FL are imported. Coverage and election specificity vary by source; other states and territories require authoritative feeds. EAC election-office directory ↗

Event schedules

Venue and promoter calendars vary by operator; no nationwide authoritative public API.

CCTV / traffic cameras

Public viewing does not establish redistribution or analytic rights. Agency terms, stable endpoints, and camera-specific provenance must be approved.

Partner & government feeds

Agency agreements, access controls, retention, and use restrictions must be established before ingestion.

Cyber asset linkage
