EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Schedule data is from ESPN’s public scoreboard and should be confirmed with the NFL or host club before action. Venue points are unreviewed candidate coordinates from community mapping sources and explicit venue crosswalks, never entrance or asset coordinates. Community mapped stadium outlines are research candidates, not operator-approved ground security perimeters. FAA SEAMS boundaries describe event airspace. FAA TFR shapes are spatial review candidates. Single explicit UTC windows from the FAA NOTAM detail are compared with listed kickoffs, but recurring, standing, and multi-area restrictions need direct review. Neither source is a ground security perimeter, drone detection, or evidence of a violation. NOAA SPC convective and WPC excessive-rainfall categories are regional forecasts at candidate points and listed kickoffs, not warnings, observed conditions, or venue impacts. Public alerts and nearby observations are context, not a threat finding or impact assessment. DHS national advisories are not venue-specific findings. Arlington police call counts are delayed public records; Seattle counts are closed CAD responses; Chicago counts are delayed geocoded historical records with known location-data limitations; Indianapolis counts are delayed public calls-for-service aggregates; Charlotte-Mecklenburg counts are delayed incident-report aggregates that include noncriminal and potentially unfounded reports. None identifies a stadium incident or threat or serves as an active police alert. CMPD currently open roadway entries are approximate area context; a nearby count does not establish route impact, a stadium incident, or a threat. MBTA Foxboro station alerts are transit service context; a kickoff time overlap does not establish game-train impact, venue access disruption, or a threat. MBTA Foxboro game-date schedules describe planned station service, not live train positions or guaranteed operation. Current MBTA station predictions are estimates; an empty check does not establish cancellation or lack of later game service. Roadway cameras are listed by distance, and published road closure windows are screened against kickoff where available; neither establishes a stadium view, travel impact, or threat. 511NJ MetLife listings and nearby game-date road entries are source planning context; a date mention does not verify a kickoff-time overlap or route impact. NFL publisher RSS headlines are matched by team-name mentions and may concern another game. ESPN game-summary preview and recap headlines are keyed to a game ID, but neither source verifies VIP attendance, event impact, or a threat. The open game receives a direct ESPN game-summary check about every five minutes while visible; its dated score, reported attendance and article metadata are publisher observations and do not verify a named person’s presence, venue impact, or a threat. The opt-in exercise uses fictional observations and deterministic correlations; it is separate from the public-source brief and is not an AI or operational threat finding. This site does not collect person records, perform scans, or operate an analyst case system.
